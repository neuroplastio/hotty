### 2026-10-22

- **V5-01** hotty 0.5 on the branch `hotty-0.5`, not released. It adds
  `a=field` (§6.4): a program changes a control unless the user has
  changed it since its `rev`, else `ESTALE`. It also adds `rev` in

  end, and positions in bytes of UTF-8. A picker's completion, which left
  the caret at the start, prompted it. SPEC, SDK.md, the vectors (eight
  host, two build, two decode) and the Python client move together.
- **V6-01** hotty 0.6 drafted, not built: the op `scroll`, which scrolls
  from its target as a key bound to a scroll action would, one of the
  host's steps per op. margin asked for it to scroll its document column
  with its own keymap, and the maintainer chose the op with §10.2's actions
  over an amount in rows. The wording for SPEC, SDK.md and the vectors is
  in `tasks/0.6.md`, and SPEC.md stays 0.5 until margin's move is approved
  or other items join it.
- **V6-02** proposed: a text field may give the program `tab`, so that a
  palette completes on Tab on a surface as it does in the cells. A host
  keeps Shift+Tab and Escape, and Tab on every other element. The
  framework for hotty apps (gov R-23) met it in its palette screen, and
  completes on `ctrl+space` meanwhile. Needs the maintainer.
- **V6-03** proposed: Enter commits a text field, in a form or not. §9
  says a text control commits when focus leaves it; hotty-blitz also
  commits on Enter outside a form, and hottytest doesn't. The framework
  for hotty apps (NEIO-15) met it, and commits on Enter itself
  meanwhile. Needs the maintainer.
- **V6-02 and V6-03** accepted by the maintainer, 2026-10-30, as
  browsers have it: Tab moves focus by default and a text field that
  binds `tab=program` gives the program Tab ("prefer browser-like model
  where default behavior is standard, but the app can still override
  it"); Enter commits a text field's `change` in a form or not ("Yes,
  like browsers"), and hottytest is to commit as hotty-blitz does. Both
  are in 0.6's Accepted, for its SPEC, SDK and vectors.
- **V6-06 and V6-07** accepted by the maintainer, 2026-10-31 ("Accept"
